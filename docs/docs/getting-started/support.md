## Support

<div class="h3-like">We offer the following support options:</div>

![image.sc forum](images/image-sc-logo.png){align=left}

Join our community at the [image.sc forum](https://forum.image.sc/) to ask questions, share ideas, and get answers from MIB users and developers.
 
![image.sc forum](images/call4help.png){align=left}

**Personal Zoom Sessions**: schedule one-on-one Zoom sessions for tailored assistance. 
For more details and to book a session, visit our main website at [mib.helsinki.fi](http://mib.helsinki.fi).

We’re committed to ensuring you get the most out of MIB. Reach out through these channels for prompt support!
